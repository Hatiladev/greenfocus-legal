2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect and store (via our backend provider, Supabase):

  • Email address
  • Name (if provided)
  • Authentication tokens (to keep you logged in)

2.2 App Usage Data

To provide focus tracking, goals, and statistics features, we store:

  • Focus session duration and history (Pomodoro sessions, streaks)
  • Goals you create and mark as complete
  • App blocking preferences (which apps you choose to block and any time limits you set)
  • Aggregated screen time statistics generated from Android's Usage Access data, when you grant this permission

2.3 AI Coach ("Zen") Conversations

If you use the Zen AI coach feature, your messages are sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate a response. We do not use these conversations to train AI models, and conversations are processed solely to provide you with a response in the moment.

2.4 Payment Information

If you subscribe to GreenFocus Premium, payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play and our subscription management provider (RevenueCat). We do not directly collect or store your credit card or payment details. RevenueCat receives a device identifier and subscription status to manage your entitlements.

2.5 Accessibility Service Data (Important)

GreenFocus uses Android's AccessibilityService API to power its core app-blocking feature. This is a sensitive Android permission, and we want to be fully transparent about how it is used:

  • What it does: When you activate a Focus Session and select apps to block, the Accessibility Service detects which app is currently open on your screen (by reading the package name of the foreground app only) so that GreenFocus can intervene if that app is on your blocked list.
  • What it does NOT do: GreenFocus does not read, record, log, transmit, or store the content of your screen, your messages, your browsing activity, your keystrokes, or any personal content within other apps. The service only checks the package name (e.g., "com.instagram.android") of the app currently in the foreground — nothing more.
  • Where this data goes: This detection happens entirely on your device. The current foreground app package name is temporarily stored in local device storage (Android SharedPreferences) to coordinate the blocking behavior between the background service and the app interface. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.
  • Why we need it: Without this permission, GreenFocus cannot detect when you open a blocked app, which is the core functionality you are choosing to use.
