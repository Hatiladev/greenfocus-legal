Privacy Policy — GreenFocus

Last updated: July 29, 2026
App: GreenFocus (digital wellbeing and focus)

In one sentence: GreenFocus collects only what it needs to work (your account, your focus time, and your device's screen time), does not sell your data, and uses Android permissions solely to block distracting apps during focus.

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

This policy describes how GreenFocus handles your personal data, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13.709/2018 — "LGPD") and applicable data protection laws.

  • Controller: [NAME OF THE RESPONSIBLE PARTY OR COMPANY]
  • Contact / Data Protection Officer: greenfocus.contact@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

a) Account data

  • Your name and email, provided by you at sign-up.
  • Your password is securely managed by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored encrypted — we never have access to your password in plain text.

b) In-app usage data (linked to your account)

  • Your goals, focus history, streak, language and preferences. These are used to show your statistics and keep your progress synced across devices.

c) Device usage statistics (screen time)

  • With the Usage Access permission, the app reads your screen time and per-app usage time to display your reports and enable blocking.
  • This raw usage data is processed on your device. Only aggregated information about your progress (e.g., focus minutes per day) is synced to your account.

d) Conversations with the Zen AI

  • When you chat with the Zen assistant, the messages you send are transmitted to our server and to the language-model provider (Groq) to generate the response. We may include your first name and aggregated productivity data to personalize the reply. Please do not share sensitive data in this chat.

3. Android permissions and why we use them

GreenFocus is a focus app. Each permission has a specific, limited purpose:

  • Accessibility Service — used solely to identify which app is currently on screen so it can block distracting apps during a focus session. We do not read your screen content, passwords, messages, or personal data.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — to measure screen time and enable blocking.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — to show the block screen over the blocked app.
  • Notifications and foreground service — to keep blocking active during focus mode and to inform you.
  • Battery optimization / auto-start — to prevent the system from killing blocking in the background.

4. How we use the data

  • Create and maintain your account and authenticate your access.
  • Provide the app's features: focus, blocking, statistics, goals and the Zen assistant.
  • Keep your progress synced across devices.
  • Improve the app's stability and experience.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. Who we share with

We share data only with providers that make the app work:

  • Supabase — authentication and storage of your account data.
  • Groq — processing the messages you send to the Zen assistant to generate responses.

These providers handle data under their own security and privacy policies.

6. Storage and retention

  • Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, the associated data is removed. To request deletion, email greenfocus.contact@gmail.com.
  • Data stored only on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app.

7. Security

We use encrypted communication (HTTPS) and providers with recognized security practices. No system is 100% infallible, but we work to protect your data.

8. Your rights

At any time, you may:

  • Confirm the existence of processing and access your data;
  • Correct incomplete or outdated data;
  • Request deletion of your data / account;
  • Request portability;
  • Withdraw consent.

To exercise these rights, email greenfocus.contact@gmail.com.

9. Children and teenagers

GreenFocus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from that age group. Parental-control features, when available, are intended for use by guardians.
